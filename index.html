The rules

I1V5X10L50C100D500M1000
  • Symbols are written largest first and added: VIII = 5 + 3.
  • A smaller symbol before a larger one is subtracted, but only IV IX XL XC CD CM.
  • I X C M repeat up to 3 times; V L D never repeat.
  • The standard range is 1 to 3,999. Larger numbers used a vinculum: a bar over a numeral multiplies it by 1,000 (V̄ = 5,000).
